# Add a subdomain {#subdomain}

text

HOW TO

1. On the bottom of the left nav, click on your name.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Click Settings.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Under Workspace, select Domains & senders.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Click **Add subdomain**.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Enter your subdomain and click **Next**.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Click the copy icon PIC next to the applicable values you need to add to your BLANK.

   SCREENSHOT

   >
   >
   >If your BLANK allows you to bulk upload the fields, you can click Export CSV to export all fields.

1. When you are done, click **I've added these records** in Coworker Campaigns to continue.

   SCREENSHOT

   >[!NOTE]
   >
   >DNS changes can take up to 30 minutes to propagate. If you see a red X instead of a green check in any of the rows, Coworker Campaigns will check every two minutes until completed.

1. When all records are validated, a new table appears at the bottom of the window. Scroll down and copy the two??? values and add them to your BLANK.

   SCREENSHOT

1. When done, click **I've added this record** in Coworker Campaigns to continue.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Enter your Sender name, Email prefix, Reply-tp name, and Reply-to email, and click **Finish and set up**.

   SCREENSHOT

   TABLE DEFINING EACH ITEM???

1. Your new subdomain appears in the list. Its status reads _In progress_, as it can take anywhere from a few minutes to two hours for the process to complete.

   SCREENSHOT

1. When the process is completed, the status changes to _Verified_.

   SCREENSHOT
