# Notification skills {#notification-skills}

Use Notification Skills in Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker to review notifications, identify action items, and manage notification preferences through conversation. Work with the notifications you already receive in the notifications panel across your Adobe CX Enterprise applications.

Summarize notifications, find specific items, and group repeated notifications into distinct issues. Open a notification in its application, mark it as read or unread, or set a reminder.

>[!AVAILABILITY]
>
>Notification Skills are available to all customers with access to Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker. No additional setup is required to use the skills.
>
>To receive notifications in [!DNL Slack], your organization must have a connected [!DNL Slack] workspace.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

Before you begin, make sure you have:

* Access to Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker.
* Access to one or more Adobe CX Enterprise applications that send you notifications.
* A connected [!DNL Slack] workspace for your organization, if you want to receive notifications in [!DNL Slack].

## Review and act on notifications {#review-and-act}

Start with a summary, then refine your request and act on a specific notification.

1. Open Coworker Chat. For instructions, see [Get started with Coworker Chat](/help/chat/ui-guide.md).
1. Enter a request in the **[!UICONTROL Ask Coworker anything]** box, then select **[!UICONTROL Send]**. For example:

   *"What needs my attention today?"*

1. Review the notifications that require action and those provided for awareness.

1. Enter a follow-up question to narrow the results. For example:

   *"Are there any failures in Adobe Journey Optimizer I should know about?"*

1. Ask for details about a notification, or open it in its application to investigate.
1. Ask to mark a notification as read or set a reminder. For example:

   *"Remind me about this notification tomorrow."*

Continue the conversation without repeating context from earlier requests. If your request could apply to multiple notifications, applications, or settings, answer the clarifying question before proceeding.

You receive a confirmation request before a change that extends beyond your request.

## Supported use cases {#supported-use-cases}

Use Notification Skills to review notifications sent to you, act on them, and manage how you receive them.

>[!NOTE]
>
>Notification Skills do not send, share, or delete notifications.

### Identify action items {#identify-action-items}

Ask which notifications require your attention across your Adobe CX Enterprise applications.

Example prompts:

* "What needs my attention today?"
* "Catch me up."
* "Anything pending for me?"

### Summarize notifications {#summarize-notifications}

Request a summary for a time period or an application. Review action items separately from notifications provided for awareness. Repeated notifications are grouped into distinct issues rather than listed individually.

Example prompts:

* "Summarize my notifications from the past week."
* "Give me an overview of my notifications from the last day."
* "Summarize my Adobe Journey Optimizer notifications."

### Find specific notifications {#find-specific-notifications}

Filter notifications by criteria such as unread status, approval requests, failures, errors, or product releases.

Example prompts:

* "Show me only unread notifications."
* "Show me my approval requests."
* "Are there any critical failure alerts I should know about?"
* "Show me release notifications for Experience Platform."

### Review notification details {#review-notification-details}

Ask for the full details of a notification as it was sent. If multiple notifications match your request, specify which one you mean.

Example prompts:

* "Tell me more about the journey notification."
* "What does this alert mean for me?"

### Act on notifications {#act-on-notifications}

Open a notification in its application, mark notifications as read or unread, or set a reminder. Choose a reminder for five minutes, 15 minutes, one hour, or tomorrow.

Example prompts:

* "Mark that notification as read."
* "Mark that notification as unread."
* "Remind me about this notification in an hour."
* "Mark all my notifications as read."

### Manage notification preferences {#manage-notification-preferences}

Ask about your current settings before changing how you receive notifications. Specify the application or notification category you want to update.

| Preference | Available changes |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery channels | Turn notifications within the application, email notifications, or [!DNL Slack] notifications on or off for an application or category. |
| Email frequency | Choose immediate email delivery or a daily or weekly digest. |
| Category priority | Choose which notification categories have high priority. |
| Pop-up alerts | Choose which notification categories display pop-up alerts. |

Example prompts:

* "What are my notification settings for Adobe Journey Optimizer?"
* "Disable [!DNL Slack] notifications for Experience Platform."
* "Switch my daily digest to weekly."
* "Mute release update notifications."
* "Only send Adobe Journey Optimizer notifications by email."
