# About Coworker Projects {#overview}

>[!AVAILABILITY]
>
>Coworker Projects is in limited availability. Contact your Adobe account representative to learn more on how to get involved.

Coworker Projects is a shared experience where practitioners use reusable workflows to complete end-to-end customer experience work. Workflows bring people, agents, skills, and business context together across Adobe CX Enterprise and third-party applications. Governance and human decisions guide the work from start to finish.

Authorized users can create, publish, and manage project templates. Practitioners use templates to start projects for business initiatives, collaborate with their team, and make decisions at approval gates.

>[!NOTE]
>
>Coworker Projects and [Coworker Chat](/help/chat/overview.md) are modules of Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker. This page covers Coworker Projects.

## Included project templates {#project-templates}

Start with an included template for a common marketing use case. Each template provides a repeatable workflow that you can adapt to your business requirements. Choose from these five templates to start a project in Coworker Projects.

### Unified audiences and orchestrated journeys {#unified-audiences-orchestrated-journeys}

Plan, launch, and measure a B2C campaign across the customer journey. Use Adobe Real-Time CDP to build and activate audiences, and Adobe Journey Optimizer to orchestrate campaigns through launch. Use Adobe Customer Journey Analytics to understand performance.

Follow a repeatable process for decisions, validation, approvals, and measurement. Apply your organization's brand, consent, legal, and governance requirements.

Required compatible applications: Adobe Workfront and Marketo Engage.

### Enterprise brief to campaign launch {#enterprise-brief-campaign-launch}

Turn a B2B campaign brief into an email campaign ready for execution. Manage planning and collaboration in Workfront, and build and prepare the campaign in [!DNL Adobe Marketo Engage].

Complete quality checks and required approvals before sending, then capture results in Workfront. Follow a repeatable path from planning through execution and measurement.

Required compatible applications: Adobe Journey Optimizer (B2C), Real-Time CDP, and [!DNL Adobe Customer Journey Analytics].

### Paid media campaign {#paid-media-campaign}

Plan, launch, and optimize paid media campaigns through a coordinated workflow. Start with objectives, budget, timing, destination, and intended outcomes in Workfront. Coordinate audience, creative, and targeting across Real-Time CDP, [!DNL Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing], and [!DNL Adobe Experience Manager Assets].

Complete validation and spend authorization before publishing. Then review optimization recommendations and close out the campaign.

Required compatible applications: [!DNL Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing], Real-Time CDP, [!DNL Adobe Experience Manager Assets], and Workfront.

### Campaign and journey health watch {#campaign-journey-health-watch}

Monitor the operational health of campaigns and journeys in Journey Optimizer. Review live journeys and their associated audiences within a configured monitoring window. Identify campaigns with failed publish, stop, or delivery events, and review likely causes and recommended next steps.

Track findings across reviews as new, still open, or resolved. Monitor issues without making changes to Journey Optimizer.

Required compatible application: Journey Optimizer.

### Experiment performance review {#experiment-performance-review}

Review experiment performance and determine what action to take. Use experimentation capabilities in Adobe Journey Optimizer and [!DNL Adobe Customer Journey Analytics] to evaluate active experiments consistently.

Capture findings and decisions during regular reviews. Identify when winning experiences are ready to move forward or underperforming experiences require action.

Required compatible applications: Journey Optimizer and Customer Journey Analytics.

## The project experience {#project-experience}

Use Coworker Projects to coordinate workflows around your business goals:

* **Workflows for your goals:** Design and deploy workflows that coordinate multiple agents, with governance and human approval gates.
* **Adaptive execution:** Trigger work through events, signals, or schedules. Coworker evaluates outcomes against your objectives and adjusts or triggers actions based on results.
* **Collaboration and approvals:** Review, approve, and act within a shared project, with context available to stakeholders throughout the workflow.
