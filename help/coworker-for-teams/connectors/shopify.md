## How to connect

1. On the [Coworker for teams homepage](https://coworker-campaigns.experience.adobe.com/), click **Customize** and select **Connectors**.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Click **Add integration**.

   SCREENSHOT

   >[!NOTE]
   >
   >If this is not your first integration, the button will read "Add connector."

1. In the Shopify row, click **Connect**.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Enter your Shopify **Workspace host**, **SQL warehouse path**, **Personal access token**, and **Unity catalogs**. Click **Connect**.

   SCREENSHOT

After connection, Shopify appears in the Connectors list and...................

**To disconnect:**

1. In the Connectors screen, find the Shopify tile and click **Manage**.

   SCREENSHOT

1. Click **Disconnect** (no need to re-enter your Personal access token at this time).

   SCREENSHOT

1. Click **Disconnect** again to confirm.
